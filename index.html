01

AI Content Editing & Humanization

AI tools generate drafts fast — but raw AI content often sounds flat, repetitive, and unconvincing to real readers. I transform AI-generated text into polished content that sounds natural, credible, and on-brand.

I remove AI "tells," fix awkward phrasing, verify facts, sharpen tone, and ensure your content meets editorial standards. This is a full editorial pass for clarity and credibility — not light proofreading. Ideal for marketing teams, startups, and agencies that need a human editor to make AI output actually work.

AI editing Humanization Fact-checking Tone refinement

02

SEO Blog Writing & Long-form Articles

Ranking on Google requires more than keyword stuffing — it demands well-researched, authoritatively written content that answers real questions and earns trust. I write SEO-optimized blog posts and long-form articles structured for both search engines and human readers.

Every piece includes keyword integration, internal linking guidance, meta descriptions, and readability optimization. Whether you need a one-off article or a consistent monthly content calendar, I deliver content that builds organic traffic over time.

SEO writing Long-form B2B articles Content calendar

03

Proofreading & Copy Editing

A single error or inconsistent tone can quietly undermine your brand's credibility. I review your content for grammar, clarity, sentence structure, tone consistency, factual accuracy, and style guide adherence — making certain it is clean, precise, and ready to publish.

Whether it's web copy, articles, or marketing materials, I refine flow and readability so your content lands as smooth, consistent, and professional.

Proofreading Copy editing Style guide Grammar

Content for Agencies

Reliable freelance writing and editing support for content, media, and branding agencies. I handle article creation, editing overflow, and white-labeled writing that is SEO-optimized, delivered on time, and tailored to meet your brand guidelines.

If your team is at capacity and you need a dependable extra pair of hands that understands editorial standards — this is built for you.
